How passes work

  • Real pays at most 2 passes per sport each day, and each pass at most once. A third pass with a game that day pays nothing.
  • Past season: pays on the anniversaries of its game days; its figures are final. Current season: its live games pay now, and the games already played pay again on their anniversaries.
  • Boosters raise live game pay only; they don't change what a pass pays on anniversaries. The app may show a boosted multiplier.
  • A whole pass bought second-hand from a past season may have used part of its limit; the app doesn't say how much.
  • Rarity raises pay per game day, not how long the pass lasts: a player pass reaches its limit after the same number of game days at any rarity.
